On the CCC2 thermostat, error code **E1** indicates a **loss of communication** between the thermostat and the system power module boards. This error causes the system to shut down.

Likely causes include:
*   Two power module DIP switches set to the same zone.
*   Issues with the communication cable.
*   Unclean 12 Volt DC power (it is suggested to try direct DC from a battery).

---

To safely discharge an RV air conditioner capacitor, you should use a **capacitor discharge tool** (also called a discharge stick) to remove the electrical current and protect yourself from shock. 

**Important Safety Information:**
* **Do not** use a screwdriver to short the terminals; this is not recommended as it can cause the capacitor to swell, explode, or destroy the terminals.
* **Do not** rely on a modern VOM (Volt-Ohm Meter) to discharge the capacitor, as their high impedance makes this process ineffective.
* Always discharge capacitors before servicing a unit, as they can remain dangerous even after the power is disconnected.

**Discharge Methods:**
* **Recommended:** Use a commercial capacitor discharge tool or a DIY tool assembled with a 2k 5-watt resistor, an insulated alligator clip, and an insulated probe.
* **Alternative (Coleman Manual):** Discharge using a 20,000 ohm (approx. 3 watt) resistor or larger, or use a standard voltmeter with a scale over 500 volts by touching the leads to each side of the capacitor.
